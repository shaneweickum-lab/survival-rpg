THORNMERE — DESIGN NOTES
========================

The bell of Thornmere has no tongue.

A village kept itself warm by forgetting. An Ember sat in the Hearth under a Crown, and the
Crown held back what the village had done long ago, at a river, under banners. When the Brood
carried the Crown away, the remembering came back all at once. People walked into the wood
and did not come out. Some of them still drift there at night, as lights.

You wake in an empty house with ash in your mouth.


PILLARS
-------
1. The body (Green Hell). Water, carbs, fats, protein, energy. Each one you neglect hurts you
   in its own way. Boil water. Cook meat. Bandage wounds. Draw venom out.
2. The hand (RuneScape). Fourteen skills on the classic XP curve, raised only by doing.
   Every level is a small fanfare. Every few levels a new tree, ore, or recipe opens.
3. The road (Oblivion). An open vale. A compass that points at the next question. Houses full
   of other people's belongings. Books that tell you the story sideways. A choice at the end
   that cannot be taken back.
4. The mind (Resolve). The dark wears you down. Fire, rest, reading and company restore you.
   At low Resolve the world desaturates and the whispers begin.


THE LOOP
--------
Gather by hand (sticks, stones, fiber, flint, berries, herbs)
  -> craft a campfire, light it (Firemaking), boil water, cook
  -> flint hatchet and pick -> logs and stone
  -> workbench (in the smithy, or build your own) -> planks, rope, bow, arrows, chest, bed
  -> bloomery -> bronze bars from copper + tin -> anvil
  -> bronze tools and sword -> iron (Mining 15) and coal (Mining 20)
  -> iron weapons and armor, cauldron, potions, building a home
  -> gold and aether (Mining 30/35) -> rings, Lantern Oil, the deep places


SKILLS AND WHAT THEY OPEN
-------------------------
Vitality     fight anything; +3 max health per level (starts at 10)
Melee        +2.5% melee damage per level; training dummy teaches to 10
Block        absorb more, spend less stamina while guarding
Marksman     bow damage and draw speed
Athletics    run faster, recover stamina faster; trained by running and jumping
Woodcutting  oak, birch, deadwood at 1; pine at 15 (bronze axe)
Mining       stone, copper, tin at 1; iron 15, coal 20 (bronze pick); gold 30, aether 35 (iron pick)
Smithing     bronze bars 1, anvil 3, bronze sword 5, iron bars 15, iron gear 16-28, rings 32+
Crafting     rope, hatchet, bandages 1; workbench 2; bow 6; leather armor 10; backpack 16; chitin 22
Cooking      meat 1; fish 5; Hearth Stew 8; Honey Cake 14. Higher level = less burning
Firemaking   faster, surer fire-lighting; fires burn longer
Foraging     more from every bush; ashleaf at 3, moonpetal (night only) at 5
Fishing      faster bites, fewer escapes
Alchemy      Bloodroot Draught 3, Ashleaf Tincture 7, Moonpetal Elixir 10, Honeyed Vigor 12, Lantern Oil 24


CREATURES
---------
Bog Toad (1)          passive, flees; meat and slime
Burrow Rat (3)        bites can cause bleeding; meat and hide
Ember Wasp (5)        flies; guards the hives of Ember Grove; stingers, honeycomb
Marsh Viper (6)       rears up angry when struck; venom
Thornback Spider (11) the Hollow, and the woods at night; silk, chitin, venom
Hollow Wisp (14)      night only, from the second night; drains Resolve; fears fire and Lantern Oil
The Broodmother (32)  Thornback Hollow. Carries the Hollow Crown.


MAIN QUEST — THE UNLIT HEARTH (spoilers)
----------------------------------------
1. Ash and Waking ............ gather sticks and stones, build and light a campfire
2. The Water Remembers ....... the bottle on the nightstand; fill at the mere, boil, drink
3. An Edge Against the World . flint hatchet, chop five logs, eat something cooked
4. What the Warden Wrote ..... the Daybook on the bookstand in the Hearth-House
5. Bronze Before Courage ..... Hadrig's Ledger (smithy), a bloomery, four bronze bars
6. The Anvil Wakes ........... raise an anvil, forge a bronze sword
7. Into the Thornback Hollow . slay the Broodmother, take the Hollow Crown
8. The Bell Without a Tongue . stand before the Hearth and choose:
     Seal the Ember   — the village forgets and is warm; the wisps go dark; Hearthward Plate
     Let the Hearth Wake — the village remembers everything; the wisps become harmless; the Ember


SIDE QUESTS AND SECRETS
-----------------------
For When We Are Big — a child's rhyme in the family house points to the Drowned Oak at the
  north shore of the mere. Papa's Box holds the Warden's Locket.
Ash for the Lantern — the Lantern-Keeper wants five Wisp Ash. Pays in a ring and coin.
The Old Barrow (far north-west) — standing stones, wisps by day, and Dawnwake, the old
  hearth-keepers' sword, deadly to the hollow dead.
Thornback Hollow — a silk-bound chest with Sunstring, a golden bow.


DEATH
-----
Your belt stays with you. Your pack stays where you fell, marked on the map. Go back for it.
